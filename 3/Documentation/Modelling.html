Question 3 · Dutch TTF · §05

Fit the level. Then test the change.

Two regressions on the closed file. Model A is the fair percent spread, given storage. Model B asks whether a storage surprise still moves that spread over the next one, five and ten sessions. The strategy and the backtest are a later note.

s12_pct = (M1 − M2) / M2 × 100   ·   s13_pct = (M1 − M3) / M3 × 100

S < 0 is contango. S > 0 is backwardation. The level model uses percent because two euros was noise near €300 and a real gap near €20. The change model stays in €/MWh: the spread h sessions ahead minus the spread today, on the same contracts.

03Model A, fair value

OLS on the 2,774 sessions. Newey–West standard errors, maximum lag 10, because the daily residual is autocorrelated. The same right-hand side for s13_pct. This table is the specification. A coefficient attached to a trading day comes from the walk-forward.

s12_pctt = α + β1 storage_devt + β2 storage_dev_sqt + β3 hdd_anom7t

+ β4 lng_devt + β5 switch_gapt + β7 inj_utilt + Σ γm Dm + δ crisist + εt

January has no dummy. crisis is 1 from 1 Jul 2021 through 31 Mar 2023. β6 would have been σ of M1. It is omitted. Estimate the same equation a second time without crisis and put the two tables side by side. The baseline keeps the dummy.

04Model B, the change

For h = 1, 5 and 10. The label is in €/MWh. The regressors do not change with h. There is no full-sample table: zt is a walk-forward residual, so the coefficients are estimated in the next section, on earlier rows only. Newey–West, maximum lag 10, at each horizon.

(s12t+h − s12t) = a + b · surprise7_zt + c · Δhdd_anom7t + d · zt + ut

The same equation for M1 minus M3, with the walk-forward residual of s13_pct and the ds13 labels. h = 5 uses ds12_fwd5 and ds13_fwd5 as they stand. Implied signs: b < 0, more injection than the weather and the stock suggested cheapens M1 against M2; d < 0, a spread that is already rich tends to fall back. A b that is still present at 5 or at 10 days means the market is slow. A wrong sign is reported.

Δhdd_anom7 is the session-to-session change in the hdd_anom7 column already stored. zt uses a Model A fit through t−1 and a window of 60 residuals that ends at t−1. Both are known at the close of t. Today’s settlement enters only as the spread being scored.

05Residual and half-life

From the walk-forward fitted value. The residual of a regression fit to the whole file is not an input. The same construction for s13_pct.

et = s12_pctt − fittedt

zt = (et − mean of the previous 60 e) / stdev of those 60

et = φ et−1 + ηt  →  half-life = −ln 2 / ln φ

The 60 residuals are the ones priced before t. The residual being scored sits outside that window. A missing residual stays missing. The z-score therefore starts on the 61st walk-forward residual. Inside each regime, estimate φ on those walk-forward residuals. When φ is between 0 and 1, report the half-life in sessions next to φ. Otherwise report φ and leave the half-life blank.

Previous 60 residuals

Mean and standard deviation. Ends at t−1. Holes are not filled with zero.

06Walk-forward

Expanding window, refit on the first session of each calendar month, on every valid row strictly before that session. Hold the coefficients until the next refit. Past residuals stay the residuals that were priced at the time.

Sample for the refit

Everything valid before the first session of the month. The first refit is the first session of January 2017, so every delivery month has already appeared. Sessions in 2016 have no walk-forward fitted value.

Days priced this month

One set of coefficients. Day t is outside the fit. Model B uses the same calendar, and its first refit waits until 60 rows have a label, surprise7_z, Δhdd and z.

The 2016–2020 line is the scored sessions from January 2017 through December 2020. Sessions from 1 Jan 2021 through 30 Jun 2021 stay in the estimation sample and receive no regime line. R² is 1 minus the residual sum of squares divided by the sum of squares around the mean of the dependent variable inside that regime. A negative R² means the walk-forward fit loses to that mean.

07Before a coefficient is quoted

Six gates. A number that fails one of them is not a result.
