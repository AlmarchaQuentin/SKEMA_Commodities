00Scope

M1, M2 and M3 are the ICE Endex generics TZT1, TZT2 and TZT3: front month, second month, third month. The dependent variables are the calendar spreads. The outright settlements stay in the file because volatility and the coal–gas switch are built from them. Coal is already €/MWh inside ttf_switch.

s12 = M1 − M2    s13 = M1 − M3    s23 = M2 − M3

s12_pct = s12 / M2 × 100    s13_pct = s13 / M3 × 100    s23_pct = s23 / M3 × 100

S < 0 is contango, S > 0 is backwardation. The level model uses the percent spreads: two euros was noise near €300 and a real gap near €20. ds12_fwd5 and ds13_fwd5 are the spread five sessions ahead minus the spread today, on the same contracts, and empty when a roll falls inside the window. They are labels.

What this step leaves alone.

No download, no second shift of the processed files, and no refit of features that already exist. The model and the backtest come after the file is saved. The old notebook that stacks rows is not this join.

01Storage-flow surprise

A cold day is supposed to withdraw gas. The surprise is the flow that remains after the heating anomaly and the stock level are accounted for. A positive value means more gas went into storage than those two implied, which is bearish for the front spread.

Inputsfunda.csv and weather_hist.csv, unshifted
Columns addedsurprise7, surprise7_z
Lagshift(2) after the sum and the z-score
  1. Recompute net_inj = −netWithdrawal / 1000 and storage_dev from raw storage. The normal is the same calendar day in the five preceding years only. Map 29 February to 28 February, and keep 29 February out of the 28 February average. The deviation starts on 1 January 2016.
  2. Recompute the daily heating anomaly from raw weather: hdd minus the same calendar day over the previous ten years, the year itself excluded, with the same February rule. Use the daily anomaly, not hdd_anom7.
  3. Place both on one gas-day table. A row enters the regression when net_inj, that day’s heating anomaly, and yesterday’s storage_dev are all present. The first such day is 2 January 2016.
  4. At each gas day, fit only on earlier days: net_inj on that day’s heating anomaly, yesterday’s storage_dev, an intercept, and calendar-month dummies, January omitted. Drop a dummy that does not vary in the past sample. Leave the expectation empty until the design has full rank.
  5. surprise7 sums net_inj minus that expectation over the day and the six preceding gas days. One missing residual leaves the sum empty.
  6. surprise7_z subtracts the mean of the previous 365 gas days and divides by their sample standard deviation, excluding the day itself. It stays empty until 365 prior values exist.
  7. Apply shift(2) on the gas-day index, the same clock as AGSI. A five-year same-day average of the flow is not written.

Surprise7 = Σk=0…6 (NetInj − E[NetInj])t−k

The processed files are the wrong pair for this regression. On a processed row, storage is gas day t−2 and realised weather is gas day t−1.

What corrupts the series.

Building the residual from funda_processed.csv and weather_hist_processed.csv. Those columns are already lagged, and they are not lagged by the same number of days.

04Checks before the file is saved

Five gates. If one fails, the file is not the sample the model should see.

  • On the unshifted gas-day table, the daily change in gasInStorage lines up with net_inj, inside revision noise.
  • After its first valid date, storage_dev has no unexplained null. In the lagged storage file that date is 3 January 2016. The blanks on 1 and 2 January are the shift(2) itself. From the first session, 4 January 2016, the trading-day series is complete.
  • fc_hdd_anom7 is empty before 15 February 2024 and present on every later session through 5 October 2026.
  • consumption, consumptionFull and trend are absent.
  • The saved targets are s12, s13, s23, the three percent spreads, and ds12_fwd5, ds13_fwd5. m1, m2 and m3 remain in the file as raw settlements.
The file is ready for the model when those five hold.

The scatter of the percent M1–M2 spread against storage_dev belongs to the model notebook, not to this step.
