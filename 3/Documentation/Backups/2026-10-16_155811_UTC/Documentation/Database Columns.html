Question 3 · Dutch TTF · Columns

What each column in the final database is.

One row of 3. Final_DB/final_db.csv is one TTF session. The lags are already in the numbers. The columns below are the ones in the file, in the order they appear.

File final_db.csv Rows 2,778 Columns 52

00How to read a row

M1, M2 and M3 are the ICE Endex generics TZT1, TZT2 and TZT3. Storage and LNG are known two gas days after the day they describe. Realised weather is known the next day. The forecast is the issue date itself. Prices are the settlement of that session.

S < 0   contango   ·   S > 0   backwardation

A positive surprise7 means more gas went into storage than the weather and the stock implied. That is bearish for the front spread. consumption, consumptionFull, trend and the contract-code columns are not in the file.

02Prices and targets

The outrights stay because volatility and the coal–gas switch are built from them. The dependent variables are the spreads.

s12 = M1 − M2    s13 = M1 − M3    s23 = M2 − M3

s12_pct = s12 / M2 × 100    s13_pct = s13 / M3 × 100    s23_pct = s23 / M3 × 100

The level model uses the percent spreads. Two euros was noise near €300 and a real gap near €20.

03Risk and the coal–gas switch

Volatility and the spread standard deviation need 20 same-contract observations. A roll blanks that day’s return, and a front month lasts about 20 sessions, so the window is clean only at the end of the contract.

196 sessions have a volatility number.

The other 2,582 are empty on purpose. Putting vol_m1 into the level regression would keep only those 196 days. Leave the five columns out of the long sample.

ttf_switch = (0.52 / 0.38) · (Coal + 0.341 · EUA) − 0.202 · EUA

Coal is already €/MWh thermal: API2 in $/t, divided by EUR/USD and by 6.978. Efficiencies are 52% for a gas plant and 38% for coal. Emission factors are 0.202 and 0.341 tCO₂ per MWh thermal.

vol_m3The same volatility for M3. Same 196 sessions. M1 should sit above M2, and M2 above M3.%

04Storage and the flow surprise

Every storage column is lagged two gas days. A Friday print that lands on Sunday sticks to Monday. The level is percent full, because working-gas volume has roughly doubled since 2011.

storage_dev = full − mean of the same calendar day in the five preceding years

surprise7 = Σk=0…6 (net_inj − expected net_inj)t−k

The expected flow is fit on earlier gas days only: that day’s heating anomaly, yesterday’s storage_dev, and calendar-month dummies. A five-year average of the flow is not in the file. 29 February uses the 28 February normal and stays out of that average.

ColumnWhat it isUnit
fullGas in storage as a percent of working-gas volume. The published AGSI figure.%
full_norm5yThe average of full on the same calendar day over the five preceding years only. The current year is not in the average.%
storage_devPercent full minus that normal, in percentage points. Positive means storage is above the seasonal level. Filled on every session in the file. This is the main storage feature.pp
storage_dev_sqThe square of storage_dev. It lets the curve steepen faster when storage is short than when it is merely above normal.pp²
net_injNet injection, −netWithdrawal / 1000. Positive on a day when gas is going into storage.TWh/d
inj_utilInjection divided by injection capacity. High utilisation means the tanks are struggling to take more gas.ratio
wd_utilWithdrawal divided by withdrawal capacity.ratio
surprise7The storage-flow surprise over seven gas days, after the two-day lag. Positive means more gas went in than the weather and the stock implied. Empty on the first seven sessions; the first value is 13 January 2016.TWh
surprise7_zsurprise7 minus its mean over the previous 365 gas days, divided by the standard deviation of those days. The day itself is not in the window. Empty until 12 January 2017, which is 266 sessions.σ

05Weather and LNG

Realised weather is lagged one day. The forecast is not lagged. LNG is lagged two days, on the same clock as storage. Degree-days are already a demand-weighted European average, base 18°C.

Do not fill the forecast with zero.

fc_hdd_anom7 is empty before 15 February 2024, and again on 6 and 7 October 2026. A zero would be read as a normal weather forecast. The earlier rows stay in the file.

cddCooling degree-days, same lag and same weighting.°C·day
