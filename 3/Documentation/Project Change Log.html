IMPLEMENTATION RECORD · 16 OCTOBER 2026

TTF storage and spread research

Executed on 2,778 sessions, 4 January 2016–7 October 2026. The pandas/NumPy notebook structure is retained. Storage improves spread-level estimates; this specification does not establish incremental five-session forecasting value.

Changes made

  1. Prices and risk: corrected expiry and delivery hours using UK business days and daylight saving, retained inferred contract IDs, added M2/M3 risk and momentum. Twenty valid non-roll observations replace the broken 20-row volatility window: all 2,778 study dates now have risk estimates.
  2. Availability and targets: storage/LNG lag two calendar days; ERA5 weather and weather-adjusted flow lag six; forecast aggregates lag one. Flow estimation requires 365 past complete days. All three spreads have 1/5/10-session targets, entering next settlement, excluding rolls and exits beyond the sample.
  3. Models: added 4. Modelling/spread_models.ipynb. Monthly expanding Ridge, fixed α=10, training-only scaling and 365 completed labels. Compared no-change, seasonality, autoregression, storage-only, autoregression plus storage, enhanced and short-history forecast specifications. M1/M3 forecasts equal the sum of adjacent euro forecasts. Added separate level analysis, matched-date comparisons, coefficients and HAC uncertainty estimates.
  4. Trading: added 4. Strategy/spread_strategy.ipynb and 5. Backtest/spread_backtest.ipynb. Fixed thresholds, no overlapping positions per account, equal-MWh legs, daily settlement P&L, cash drawdowns, yearly results and unchanged trades at 1×/2×/4× assumed costs. The three spreads are standalone accounts, not an independent portfolio.
  5. Trace and rerun: run_project.py executes nine notebooks without downloads; validate_project.py checks timing, identities and cash reconciliations. Source hashes, package versions and execution records are saved in Documentation/run_manifest.json; 91 independent checks passed in Documentation/validation_results.json. Original design pages are retained and marked as superseded.

Limits and next decision

Contract mappings are inferred from Bloomberg generics; storage/LNG and ERA5 histories contain revisions. Exact forecast run times and genuine market-consensus storage surprises are unavailable. The unverified coal/switch feature is excluded. Fractional-MW positions, costs and margin capital are research assumptions. Four one-session accounts exhaust capital at 4× costs; their Sharpe is unavailable. Forecast weather supports only 88 one-session prediction dates; five- and ten-session forecast tests lack the required history. Obtain verified contract histories and publication vintages before execution claims, then test a pre-specified storage/forecast-news hypothesis on newly reserved data.
